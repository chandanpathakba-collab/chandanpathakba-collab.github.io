AEC/O · Digital Twin · 2023–Present
From Reactive to Predictive: Digital Twin Platform for Global Facilities
The Problem
Global facility owners were spending millions on reactive maintenance — fixing things after they broke, with no unified intelligence layer for asset health, ESG compliance, or cost forecasting across multi-site portfolios. The market had legacy incumbents (Autodesk, IBM Maximo, Planon) but none offered open, interoperable, modern AEC/O workflows.
My Approach
Owned full product strategy at Nemetschek Group — defining multi-year roadmap, competitive positioning, and global stakeholder alignment. Identified predictive maintenance and ESG analytics as the strategic wedge where incumbents were weakest. Championed OPEN BIM as a differentiator for enterprise adoption. Designed inclusive UX for field operators including non-technical and elderly users. Delivered real-time ROI and sustainability dashboards — giving portfolio owners intelligence they'd never had before.
Outcome
🏆 CEO Certificate of Excellence 2025 Multi-year roadmap shipped OPEN BIM standard led Predictive maintenance live ESG dashboards delivered Field UX for all user types

Insurance · B2B SaaS · 2017–2023
$10M Saved Annually: Rebuilding GEICO's Digital Insurance Platform
The Problem
GEICO's digital insurance workflows had accumulated years of technical and UX debt — fragmented acquisition funnels, low engagement, high drop-off rates, and a security posture exposing the business to major cyber risk. Cost per acquisition was high and referral loops were broken.
My Approach
Led ground-up strategic redesign as Senior Domain Lead — owning roadmap, UX direction, pricing model, and GTM in partnership with C-suite, engineering, and sales. Implemented outcome-led roadmapping where every initiative tied directly to a business metric. Redesigned the acquisition funnel and referral mechanic using customer discovery and behavioural data. Defined a new security and DR strategy, eliminating a major cyber risk class entirely.
Outcome
$5–$10M annual savings +15% customer acquisition +10% user engagement +5% referral sales −60% cyber risk exposure

Automotive · InsurTech · 2016–2017
+56% Retention: Usage-Based Insurance via Telematics
The Problem
Traditional flat-rate auto insurance was losing customers to more personalised competitors. Insurers needed a behaviour-driven pricing model that rewarded safe drivers and improved retention without sacrificing premium yield or alienating the existing policyholder base.
My Approach
Designed a UBI framework at Ness Technologies using telematics data — speed patterns, braking behaviour, mileage, and time-of-day driving — to build dynamic per-driver risk profiles. Partnered with data science and engineering to turn raw telemetry into real-time underwriting models. Key insight: advocated for a behaviour-reward mechanism (safe driver discounts) over pure risk-penalty — dramatically improving opt-in rates and creating loyalty loops that compounded over time.
Outcome
+56% customer retention YoY Telematics data pipeline built Dynamic UBI pricing live Registration analytics shipped Reward-based loyalty model

Capital Markets · Enterprise SaaS · 2009–2016
Fortune 500 Proxy Platform Transformation — 3 Continents
The Problem
Broadridge's financial proxy voting infrastructure — used by Fortune 500 clients across North America, Europe, and APAC — was fragmented, compliance-heavy, and falling behind cross-border regulatory demands. Users struggled to navigate it, compliance teams were overburdened, and cross-regional feature gaps were creating commercial risk with major accounts.
My Approach
Over 7 years, grew from individual contributor to Team Lead, driving a multi-phase platform transformation. Led full discovery, requirements, and delivery of user-centric features across three continents. Key contribution: championing a user-journey-first mindset in a compliance-heavy environment — reframing from "what the regulation requires" to "what the user needs to comply easily." This shift improved usability, reduced support burden, and strengthened client relationships. Built and mentored a BA team, raising delivery quality across concurrent workstreams.
Outcome
Fortune 500 client base retained 3-continent feature rollout Compliance posture improved BA team built & mentored User-first culture established
